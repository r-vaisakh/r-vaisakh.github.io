About

I am an observational cosmologist working on large-scale structure, galaxy–halo connections, survey simulations, and precision cosmology.

My research uses spectroscopic galaxy surveys and cosmological simulations to understand how galaxies trace the underlying dark matter distribution and how large-scale structure can be used to constrain the expansion and growth history of the Universe.

I am a member of the Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument (DESI) Collaboration, where my work includes mock-catalog production, clustering analyses, observational systematics, and survey operations.
