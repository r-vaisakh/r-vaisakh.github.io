About

I am an observational cosmologist working on large-scale structure, galaxy–halo connections, spectroscopic survey cosmology, and cosmological simulations.

My research combines galaxy surveys and numerical simulations to understand how galaxies and quasars trace the underlying dark matter distribution, and how their clustering can be used to constrain cosmological models.

I am a Builder in the Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument (DESI) Collaboration. My work includes QSO and ELG mock-catalog production, galaxy–halo connection modelling, clustering validation, redshift-failure studies, observational systematics, and survey operations.

Research Interests

Large-scale structure · Galaxy–halo connection · Cosmological simulations · Mock catalogs · Spectroscopic surveys · BAO/RSD · Higher-order clustering · Observational systematics · Simulation-based inference
