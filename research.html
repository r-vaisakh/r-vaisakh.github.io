Research

Large-Scale Structure & Galaxy–Halo Connection

I study how galaxies and quasars trace the underlying dark matter distribution, with a focus on connecting observed clustering to their host dark matter halos.

My work uses DESI observations together with high-resolution cosmological simulations and mock galaxy catalogs to model the clustering of quasars and emission-line galaxies across a broad redshift range.

Survey Simulations & Precision Cosmology

I develop and validate high-fidelity mock catalogs for large spectroscopic surveys, including reference lightcones and covariance mocks based on simulations such as Uchuu and GLAM.

These simulations support measurements of baryon acoustic oscillations, redshift-space distortions, full-shape clustering, and studies of observational systematics in DESI.

Nearby-Universe Cosmology with Supernovae

I also work on stripped-envelope core-collapse supernovae observed with the ROTSE-III telescope network, using the expanding photosphere method to estimate distances in the nearby Universe.

This work explores an independent route toward measuring the local cosmic expansion and testing the consistency of cosmological distance measurements across different redshift regimes.

Observatory Operations & Instrumentation

My research experience also includes astronomical observing, survey operations, data-quality monitoring, and instrumentation development for near-infrared astronomical applications.
